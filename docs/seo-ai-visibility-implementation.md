# SEO, AEO, GEO, LLMO, AISEO, and E-E-A-T implementation

## SEO foundation

- Canonical URLs on indexable generated pages
- Valid slugs return 200 and invalid slugs return 404
- Permanent redirect from `/contact` to canonical `/contact-us`
- Sitemap contains canonical public routes and excludes private, redirected, and invalid URLs
- Robots rules protect admin, API, influencer, and private landing areas
- Crawlable internal links between hubs, guides, trust pages, and conversion routes
- Descriptive metadata, Open Graph, Twitter cards, and stable modified dates
- Mobile-responsive shared design and consistent Inter font

## AEO

- Direct answer near the top of each generated page
- Descriptive question and task headings
- Step-by-step workflows and quality checks
- Twelve to fifteen visible, page-relevant FAQs
- FAQ structured data that matches visible content
- Concise definitions, limitations, and next actions

## GEO and LLMO

- Stable organization, website, editorial team, and ownership entities
- Public editorial policy and correction path
- `OAI-SearchBot` and `PerplexityBot` public access while private routes remain blocked
- `llms.txt` with canonical site guidance and interpretation limitations
- Source-aware language and preference for official documentation
- Self-contained answer blocks that retain context when summarized
- No fabricated local presence, authors, ratings, counts, earnings, or guarantees

## AISEO and AISO

- Traditional and AI-search requirements are treated as one publishing system rather than separate keyword layers
- Page templates prioritize user intent, unique value, factual review, entity clarity, technical access, and measurable outcomes
- Generated scale is controlled through valid datasets and real 404 handling

## E-E-A-T

- ThinkGrow Pvt. Ltd. ownership context
- Sikhadenge Editorial Team organizational author page
- Public editorial and AI-assisted content policy
- Visible update context and correction route
- Human accountability for final published content
- High-stakes limitations and qualified-source requirements
- Removal of unsupported ratings, member counts, urgency, earnings, placement, and ranking claims

## Limits

These changes improve technical eligibility and content comprehension. They do not guarantee crawling, indexing, rankings, rich results, AI citations, traffic, leads, jobs, or income.

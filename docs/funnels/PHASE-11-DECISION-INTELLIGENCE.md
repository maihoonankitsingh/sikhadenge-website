# Phase 11 — Revenue & Decision Intelligence

## Objective

Turn Funnel v2 from a conversion tracker into a decision system. The admin layer must answer, without fabricated numbers:

- Which acquisition path creates the highest-quality customers?
- Where is the largest measurable funnel leak?
- Which leads need action now?
- Which campaigns/ad IDs create workshop and AI Expert buyers, not merely registrations?
- How quickly are WhatsApp and human follow-ups happening?
- Which reminder step is failing to deliver/read?
- How much verified gross/net revenue is attributable to each cohort?
- Are refunds or payment failures materially changing economics?

## Source-of-truth rules

1. `FunnelEvent` is the first-party behavioral/attribution ledger.
2. `FunnelPayment` is the payment-attempt/provider-status ledger.
3. `FunnelCrmProfile` is operational state only; it cannot manufacture verified purchases or revenue.
4. Revenue is created from verified purchase/conversion events and reduced only by verified refund events.
5. Missing Meta spend is shown as missing. CPL, CAC and ROAS are not estimated.
6. Visitors are globally deduplicated in the activity summary instead of summing cohort-level visitor counts.

## Two reporting truths

### Activity / Cash View — `/admin/funnel-dashboard`

The selected range filters events by the date the event happened. Use this view for:

- cash/revenue activity during the period,
- current WhatsApp and CRM workload,
- payment failures,
- recent conversion leakage,
- operational decisions.

An older lead purchasing today belongs in today's cash/activity view.

### Acquisition Cohort View — `/admin/funnel-cohorts`

The selected range filters leads by their `generate_lead` date, then follows those same lead IDs through all downstream events available up to now. Use this view for:

- Free vs Paid lead quality,
- ChatGPT vs Claude lead quality,
- campaign/ad customer quality,
- lifetime-to-date revenue per acquired lead,
- downstream workshop/core conversion.

This prevents a current-period lead denominator from being mixed with revenue generated by older leads.

Cohort maturity is shown as median age plus 7-day, 14-day and 30-day mature lead counts. Recent cohorts should not be compared blindly with mature cohorts.

Spend-derived metrics remain intentionally unavailable until a verified Meta Ads reporting source is connected.

## Operational SLA metrics

Defaults are configurable:

- `FUNNEL_WHATSAPP_SLA_MINUTES` — default 5 minutes from lead creation to first WhatsApp sent event.
- `FUNNEL_FIRST_CONTACT_SLA_MINUTES` — default 15 minutes from lead creation to first `working_lead` event.
- `FUNNEL_REMINDER_SEND_SLA_MINUTES` — default 5 minutes around a scheduled reminder send time.

The dashboard reports:

- median lead → WhatsApp time,
- % within WhatsApp SLA,
- median lead → first human contact time,
- % within first-contact SLA,
- unassigned open leads,
- overdue follow-ups,
- follow-ups due in the next 24 hours,
- high-priority open leads,
- advisor follow-ups pending,
- leads with no human-contact signal.

## Automatic bottleneck detection

For each cohort and the global activity view, the API evaluates sequential stages with at least five entrants and reports the lowest observed progression rate among:

- visitor → registration,
- lead → WhatsApp sent,
- WhatsApp sent → delivered,
- lead → live joined,
- live joined → 60-minute retained,
- live offer → workshop page,
- workshop page → checkout,
- workshop checkout → purchase,
- workshop purchase → attendance,
- core offer → checkout,
- core checkout → enrollment.

This is descriptive, not a fabricated benchmark. It identifies the largest observed conversion constraint in the selected data.

## Campaign/ad decision tables

Activity and cohort views preserve original attribution fields including source, medium, campaign, campaign ID and ad ID.

The dashboard can therefore show:

- visitors/leads,
- entry buyers,
- workshop buyers,
- AI Expert buyers,
- lead → core conversion,
- net tracked revenue,
- revenue per lead,

No spend, CPL, CAC or ROAS is displayed until a verified Meta Marketing/Ads reporting source is connected.

## WhatsApp follow-up intelligence

Route: `/admin/funnel-followups`

The existing authenticated status callback remains backward-compatible. It now optionally accepts:

- `messageType` (or `message_type` / `step`),
- `journeyStage` (or `journey_stage`),
- `templateName` (or `template_name`),
- `automationId` (or `automation_id`),
- `scheduledFor` (or `scheduled_for`).

Recommended tracking taxonomy:

- `registration_confirmation`
- `reminder_24h`
- `reminder_2h`
- `reminder_15m`
- `live_join_link`
- `post_live_workshop_offer`
- `workshop_confirmation`
- `workshop_reminder_24h`
- `workshop_reminder_2h`
- `workshop_reminder_15m`
- `advisor_followup`
- `core_enrollment_confirmation`

For each observed message step the console reports unique messages/leads, effective sent, delivered, read, failed, delivery rate, read rate, failure rate, median send delay and send-within-SLA rate.

Callbacks that do not yet include step metadata remain visible as `unclassified`; the console exposes classification coverage rather than pretending coverage is complete.

## Payment health

Payment attempts are separated by purpose:

- `masterclass_entry`
- `implementation_workshop`
- `core_program`

The activity dashboard reports attempt count, captured count, failed count, pending count, refunded count, capture rate, failure rate and top provider failure reasons.

Razorpay documentation confirms `payment.captured` and `order.paid` represent captured payment state, while `refund.processed` is the reliable final refund-state webhook used for ledger reconciliation:

- https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/payments/
- https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/orders/
- https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/refunds/

The browser tracker already pushes every funnel event into `dataLayer`, while Meta Pixel/CAPI use the same event ID for supported browser/server deduplication.

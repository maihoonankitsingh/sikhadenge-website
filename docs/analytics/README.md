# SikhaDenge Analytics and Growth OS

This directory defines the canonical measurement system for SikhaDenge.

No dashboard, event, database field, campaign report, counsellor report, or
advertising optimization should introduce a conflicting metric definition.

## Documents

- `metric-constitution.md`: canonical business and marketing KPIs
- `funnel-stage-contract.md`: exact lifecycle and funnel stages
- `data-source-authority.md`: controlling source for each metric
- `data-quality-guardrails.md`: minimum quality thresholds and alerts

## Operating principle

The final business source of truth is verified operational data:

1. verified leads
2. verified workshop activity
3. verified admissions and payments
4. verified refunds
5. verified learning and student outcomes

Advertising-platform reported conversions are optimization signals, not the
final finance or business source of truth.

# SikhaDenge Metric Constitution

This document defines the official business, marketing, workshop, sales, revenue, and student-success metrics for SikhaDenge.

No dashboard, campaign report, counsellor report, or advertising platform should introduce conflicting metric definitions.

## 1. Primary Business Metrics

### Net Paid Enrollments

Distinct valid enrollments with verified collected payment.

Exclude test, duplicate, fraudulent, cancelled, fully refunded, and reversed records.

### Collected Revenue

Successfully captured payment value.

Do not include created orders, failed payments, pending payments, unpaid admissions, or future EMI commitments.

### Net Collected Revenue

Collected revenue minus completed refunds, chargebacks, and payment reversals.

### Customer Acquisition Cost

Attributable marketing spend divided by net paid enrollments.

CAC must always mention the attribution model and reporting window.

### Marketing Efficiency Ratio

Total net collected revenue divided by total marketing spend.

### Contribution Margin

Net collected revenue minus payment fees, refunds, marketing spend, sales commissions, and variable delivery cost.

## 2. Acquisition Metrics

### Qualified Landing Session

A valid non-bot landing-page session that is not internal traffic, test traffic, or a duplicate technical page load.

### Registration Conversion Rate

Unique valid registrations divided by qualified landing sessions.

### Valid Lead Rate

Valid unique leads divided by submitted registrations.

### Cost per Valid Lead

Attributable marketing spend divided by valid unique leads.

### Contact Rate

Connected leads divided by valid assigned leads.

### Qualification Rate

Qualified leads divided by connected leads.

### Cost per Qualified Lead

Attributable marketing spend divided by qualified leads.

## 3. Workshop Metrics

### Confirmed Registrant
A valid lead assigned to a specific workshop batch or session.

### Unique Attendee
A confirmed registrant with verified workshop join activity.

### Show-Up Rate
Unique attendees divided by confirmed registrants.

### Engaged Attendee
Initially: attendance of at least 45 minutes or at least 50 percent.

### Workshop Engagement Rate
Engaged attendees divided by unique attendees.

### Attendee-to-Enrollment Rate
Net paid enrollments attributed to the workshop divided by unique attendees.

## 4. Counselling and Sales Metrics

### First Response Time
Time between lead creation and the first valid counsellor contact attempt.

### Contact Rate
Connected leads divided by valid assigned leads.

### Counselling Completion Rate
Completed counselling sessions divided by booked counselling sessions.

### Qualified Lead Close Rate
Net paid enrollments divided by qualified leads.

### Lost Lead Rate
Leads marked lost divided by workable leads.

Every lost or disqualified lead must have a controlled reason code.

## 5. Payment Metrics

### Gross Booked Revenue
Value of valid created orders, including unpaid orders.

### Payment Success Rate
Successful distinct payment attempts divided by valid payment attempts.

### Refund Rate by Enrollment
Refunded enrollments divided by paid enrollments.

### Refund Rate by Value
Refunded amount divided by collected revenue.

## 6. Student Success Metrics

### Activated Student
A paid student who completes the first successful LMS login and starts the first lesson.

### Seven-Day Activation Rate
Students activated within seven days divided by eligible paid students.

### Course Completion Rate
Students completing required course criteria divided by activated eligible students.

### Student Outcome Rate
Students with a verified defined outcome divided by eligible activated students.

Course completion and outcome achievement must remain separate metrics.

## 7. Attribution Views

Reports must preserve these attribution views separately:

- first touch
- lead-creation touch
- last non-direct touch
- last paid touch
- self-reported source
- advertising-platform attribution

One attribution model must never overwrite another.

## 8. Standard Exclusions

Unless shown separately, exclude:

- internal team activity
- test records
- bots and spam
- duplicate technical events
- invalid forms
- failed payments
- cancelled orders
- fraudulent records
- fully refunded enrollments
